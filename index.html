OUR VISION & PHILOSOPHY

Guided Steps, Blessed Futures

Novus Via Academy was established to provide families in Pretoria with a trustworthy, structured and welcoming educational environment for learners from Gr RR to Gr 7.

Our educational philosophy centres on the conviction that strong academic foundations are built through clear instruction, consistent daily routine, smaller learning groups and genuine personal encouragement.

    GOVERNANCE & DATA PROTECTION

    Privacy Policy, POPIA & Terms

    1. Privacy Policy & POPIA Information

    Novus Via Academy respects the privacy of parents, guardians and learners in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa. We collect only the personal contact and learner grade details voluntarily submitted through our admissions enquiry forms for the sole purpose of communicating with prospective families, providing requested fee structures, scheduling Centre visits and processing enrolment enquiries.

    Submitted records are stored securely with restricted administrative access and are never sold, rented or disclosed to unauthorised third parties. Parents may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their enquiry information at any time by emailing admin@novusvia.co.za.

    2. Website Terms and Conditions

    The content on www.novusvia.co.za is provided for general informational and admissions enquiry purposes regarding Novus Via Academy in Claremont, Pretoria. While every effort is made to keep information current, programme details and space availability are confirmed directly with parents during the admissions process.

    3. Enrolment Terms Overview

    Submitting an online enquiry or requesting a fee structure does not automatically reserve a learner space. Formal placement for Gr RR–Gr 7 is confirmed upon completion of the official registration documentation, parent consultation and acceptance of the Centre's code of conduct and fee agreement.
